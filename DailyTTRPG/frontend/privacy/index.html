Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how DailyTTRPG collects, uses, and protects information for its asynchronous tabletop-inspired RPG service, including SMS gameplay messaging and future web or app interfaces.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use DailyTTRPG, we may collect:

  • Name, account information, or tester information you provide.
  • Your phone number when you opt in to SMS gameplay messages.
  • SMS message content, including replies, choices, commands, and support requests.
  • Gameplay actions, character data, campaign data, world-state data, and progress history.
  • Technical logs such as device, browser, request, delivery, error, and security logs.

How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide gameplay messages, adventure prompts, reminders, and campaign updates.
  • Process player actions and advance characters, campaigns, and shared-world state.
  • Create, maintain, troubleshoot, and secure accounts and gameplay sessions.
  • Improve the service, test new features, and understand early product usage.
  • Detect abuse, protect service reliability, and comply with legal obligations.

SMS Privacy

SMS opt-in data and consent will not be sold or shared with third parties for their marketing purposes. SMS-related data may be shared with service providers and telecommunications carriers as necessary to deliver messages.

DailyTTRPG may use service providers, including Twilio and telecommunications providers, to send, route, deliver, and support SMS messages. These providers may process SMS-related information only as needed to provide messaging services and related support.

Data Retention

We retain information as needed to operate DailyTTRPG, maintain gameplay continuity, troubleshoot issues, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and improve platform reliability. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of data and the needs of the service.

Your Choices

  • You may reply STOP to opt out of DailyTTRPG SMS messages.
  • You may reply HELP for messaging help.
  • You may contact dailyttrpg@gmail.com with privacy, account, or data questions.

Children

DailyTTRPG is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as DailyTTRPG develops. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact dailyttrpg@gmail.com.
