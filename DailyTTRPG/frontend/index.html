Platform pillars

A living campaign platform with structure under the hood.

Async by design

Built for busy schedules.

DailyTTRPG is designed around asynchronous play. Players act when they can, action windows keep the adventure moving, and catch-up summaries help everyone stay oriented.

  • No live session required
  • No scheduling bottleneck
  • Action windows are native
  • Catch-up is part of the system

Authored content

Authored stories, not disposable AI improv.

Campaign content is intentionally written and structured. DailyTTRPG prioritizes authored scenes, clues, characters, and consequences over unbounded generation.

  • Rich story intent
  • Authored scenes
  • Validated content
  • Deliberate mystery, pacing, and consequence

Structured world memory

The world remembers.

DailyTTRPG tracks world truth, character knowledge, clues, NPCs, objectives, consequences, and changing scene state. The story does not reset between messages or sessions.

  • World truth
  • Character knowledge
  • Clues and consequences
  • Persistent campaign state

Human GM hooks

Human GMs can shape the world.

DailyTTRPG is not about replacing every GM. Human GMs and creators can seed hooks, edit content, override outcomes, create scenes, and guide the direction of a living campaign.

  • Seed hooks
  • Edit and override
  • Create campaigns
  • AI assists, humans can author

Natural actions

Write naturally. Resolve structurally.

Players can describe what their character does in natural language. The system interprets intent with minimal AI assistance, then validates outcomes against authored content, rules, and world state.

  • Natural player input
  • Structured validation
  • No invented outcomes
  • Content and world state are source of truth

Persistent identity

Characters that persist.

Characters are not disposable chat sessions. They can grow across adventures, worlds, and campaigns, carrying history, knowledge, relationships, and consequences with them.

  • Character continuity
  • Advancement
  • History
  • Cross-campaign potential

Character perspective

See only what your character knows.

DailyTTRPG is built around character perspective. If your character has not seen it, learned it, or discovered it, it should not appear as player knowledge.

  • Roleplay depth
  • Limited perspective
  • Character-specific knowledge
  • Mystery preservation

Approachable depth

Easy to enter. Deep if you want it.

New players can focus on the story and describe what they want to do. Veterans can inspect the underlying rules, character mechanics, checks, resources, and campaign systems when they want more control.

  • Approachable narrative layer
  • Rules available underneath
  • Accessible for new players
  • Tabletop-style depth for veterans

Web first

The browser is the main table.

The player app at /play is the primary experience for account access, characters, campaign state, and future rich adventure tools.

Optional notifications later

Optional notifications may help players know when a new beat is ready. SMS is not the primary gameplay surface.
